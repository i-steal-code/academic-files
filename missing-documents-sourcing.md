# H2 chemistry: where missing documents might be found or bought

Scope: the gaps listed in the chemistry "missing documents" list in `README.md`, plus the ones found on 2026-11-27. This is a sourcing guide. Nothing here has been downloaded, and nothing has been filed.

Status key:
- **Verified** = the source was opened or confirmed to exist during the search.
- **Unverified** = a search snippet pointed to it, but its content or legitimacy is not confirmed.

## 1. Ground rules

1. **Prefer the school first.** RI papers, prelims and notes are usually shared internally through the school's SLS or by the teacher, not posted publicly.
2. **Check provenance before filing.** Anonymous uploads on Scribd, Studocu, Cliffsnotes and Coursesidekick are often unofficial. Check the school name, year and paper number on the first page against the filename.
3. **Copyright.** Past papers, prelims and school notes are usually copyrighted. Use them for personal study. Do not redistribute them outside the library.
4. **Never enter credentials into a site to get a file.** If a source needs a login, ask the school or the owner for access. Do not share an account.
5. **Only file what matches a gap.** Each file goes into the `README.md` naming pattern. Duplicates are checked by hash first.

### G. Data booklet and syllabus

- **Data booklet:** not found in the search. It is usually issued with the exam papers. Check the TYS or prelim packs for an included booklet before looking elsewhere.

## 3. How to ask a teacher or school (template)

> Hi [teacher], I'm working through the H2 chemistry library and am missing the [paper name / lecture notes for Chapter N]. Could you share it through SLS, or tell me where it is on the school portal? Happy to use whatever format you prefer. Thanks.

Keep it specific: name the year, paper number and chapter. This gets a faster answer than a general request.

## 4. Buying

- Buy from a named seller that gives a receipt and a school name on the paper.
- Avoid sellers who offer "every H2 chemistry paper" bundles with no school names. These are often unofficial.
- Check the licence terms if the document is marked "for personal use" or "not for redistribution".
- Record each purchase in `agents/chemistry.md` with the seller, date and item.

## 6. Recording a find

When a document is found and filed:
1. Check the file's hash against any existing copy.
2. Name it by the `README.md` rules.
3. Convert it with `tools/convert_one.ps1 -Subjects "H2 chemistry"`.
4. Update the inventory in `README.md` and log it in `agents/chemistry.md`.
