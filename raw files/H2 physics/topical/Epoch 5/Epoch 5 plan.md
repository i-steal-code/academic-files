# Physics Epoch 5 · P1 diagnostic marathon

> **Frozen 2026-11-27 (A-levels over).** Kept as a practice record. Both parts were sat (60/97); S/E/G tags and miss causes were never reported.

A warm-up before the four P1 rehearsals. It has 97 MCQs and covers every chapter and every syllabus concept that can be tested by MCQ. It's in two parts, and each part follows syllabus order.

Files:
- `Epoch 5 Questions.pdf`: item numbers only.
- `Epoch 5 Answer Scheme.pdf`: the answer key on page 1, then each item's answer, source, concept, and the worked solution or a pointer to it.

Builder: `topical/build_epoch5.py`.

Sources: the 2026 NYJC P1, the RI P1s from 2021–2023, the 2025 RI timed practice MCQs, and Section A of the 2023 RI common test and the 2024 RI timed practice. E5-65 comes from the 2025 RI P1, which you sat in September; it's the only MCQ on rectification. Nine concepts have no MCQ in any of those papers, so I wrote those questions myself; they're marked "Original question" on the answer scheme. None of the four rehearsal papers (VJC, HCI, DHS, EJC) is used.

## How to sit it

1. Sit Part A, take a 10-minute break (water, stand up, no reviewing), then sit Part B. Time each part separately.
2. Work at P1 pace: about 2 minutes per question, with two passes. Answer what you can settle in about 90 s and circle the rest. Guess anything still blank at the end of each part.
3. Write your letter and S, E or G (sure, educated guess, guess) for each item on a separate sheet.
4. Mark both parts against the key. For each miss, look up its concept on the key and tag the cause: C content, R reading, N numbers, T trap, P pace.
