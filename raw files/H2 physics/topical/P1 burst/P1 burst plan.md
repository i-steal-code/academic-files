## How to sit each paper

1. **Time it to 60 minutes**, at the same time of day as P1 if you can. Shade your answers on a sheet or write them in a list, not on the question paper.
2. **Two passes.** On the first pass, answer anything you can settle within about 90 seconds. Circle the rest and move on. On the second pass, go back to the circled questions. At 55 minutes, guess any question still blank: a blank always scores 0, and a guess scores 1 a quarter of the time.
3. **Tag each answer** S (sure), E (educated guess) or G (guess), as you did on the RI P1 in September.
4. **Mark it straight after.** For every miss, write the question number and one cause:
   - **C:** content (you didn't know it)
   - **R:** reading (you missed a word or a condition, or answered a different question)
   - **N:** numbers (calculator, power of ten, units)
   - **T:** trap (you picked a distractor built from a common slip)
   - **P:** pace (rushed, or never reached)

## MCQ habits (read before each paper)

- **Cover the options first.** For a calculation, work out your answer, then look for it. Seeing the options first pulls you towards a distractor.
- **Eliminate.** Cross out options that have the wrong units, the wrong sign or the wrong order of magnitude, or that give an impossible result in a limiting case (zero mass, infinite resistance, θ = 0 or 90°).
- **Distractors are built from the usual slips:** a missing factor of 2 or ½, sine for cosine, radius for diameter, a missing square, an inverted ratio. If your answer sits next to its "slip twin", check which one you made.
- **Ratio questions:** write the formula, cancel what's constant, then scale. Don't plug in numbers.
- **Graph options:** check the intercept, the shape (the gradient changes), and the value at one point.
- **Statement questions ("which are correct?"):** judge each statement on its own, and mark it ✓ or ✗ next to it before you look at the option combinations.
- **Don't change an answer** on the second pass unless you find an actual error in your first working.
