## How it is marked

- 1 mark per point. **3 marks means 3 links.**
- **If the question asks for N things, give N.** Extra or contradictory points can cancel a right one. Put your strongest answer first.
- **No abbreviations in written answers.** Write "principle of conservation of momentum", "kinetic energy", "potential difference". Not PCM, N2L, K.E., p.d., or arrows.
- **Refer to the set-up:** name the given object, label or value.
- An equation supports an explanation; it does not replace one.
- A correct statement with a wrong reason scores 0 (2022 P2 Q3).

## Definitions: 3–8 one-mark gifts per paper

In 2025: principle of moments, centre of gravity, critical damping, principle of superposition, e.m.f., isotopes, inertia. In 2024: ideal gas, internal energy, exponential decrease. Your weak ones, word for word:

- **Gravitational potential:** work done per unit mass by an external force in bringing a small test mass **from infinity to that point**.
- **r.m.s. current:** the steady direct current that produces heat at the same average rate as the a.c. in the same resistor.
- **Longitudinal wave:** oscillations parallel to the direction of **energy transfer**.
- **e.m.f.:** energy converted from other forms to electrical energy per unit charge passing through the source.
- **Exponential decrease:** the rate of decrease is proportional to the quantity itself.
- **Internal energy of an ideal gas:** random kinetic energy only; the potential energy is zero because there are no intermolecular forces.
- **Critical damping:** returns to equilibrium in the shortest time without oscillating.
