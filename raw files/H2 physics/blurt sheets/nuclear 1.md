ch 20 nuclear

nuclide notation; isotope / nucleon number / proton number
nuclide notation gives atomic symbol, nucleon number and proton number of an element. isotopes are elements that bear the same number of protons but a different combination of neutrons. nucleon number includes protons and neutrons count in a nucleus 

mass defect and binding energy; BE per nucleon idea
mass defect is the difference between the masses of individual nucleons and the actual mass of the nucleus. binding energy is the amount of energy needed to separate each nucleon from its nucleus to infinity. 

decay equations: alpha, beta-, beta+, gamma; balance A and Z
alpha decay shoots helium nuclei out (has a +2e charge), beta- are electrons, and beta+ are positrons while gamma is photons (essentially pure energy). balance equations by adding correct respective nuclear decay particles in the products to balance the neucleons and protons on both sides of the equation. 

activity A = lambda N; half-life link; exponential decay of N or A
half-life is the amount of time taken for radioactive substance to reduce to half of its current amount. activity is the rate of decay events occurring. rest of half-life and decay formula is on the data booklet

background count: why subtract
geiger-meters read background radiation, which add systematic error. This systematic zero-error needs to be measured and accounted for when taking experimental data by subtracting background count from final readings. 

random and spontaneous: exam meanings
random decay means that decay can randomly be an increase or decrease in substance, while spontaneity refers to the random probability of a decay event happening of its own accord. All radiactive decay disregards external factors and plays by its own rules.
