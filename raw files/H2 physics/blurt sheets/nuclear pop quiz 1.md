1. Photoelectric equation linking hf, work function, and max KE.

2. What does increasing intensity change? What does it not change (below/above threshold)?
increasing intensity increases the amount of photons 

3. Threshold frequency: relation to work function.
threshold frequency is the frequency required to strip electroncs from the element completely for photoelectric effect. at threshold, hf = work function

4. Photon hits atom: when is there excitation? What happens to leftover photon energy if hf is not exactly a level gap?
when photon hits atom, there is excitation when the discrete value of energy matches the amount of energy required to jump to another level. in the event that the photon energy does not exactly match any level transition, the photon simply passes and nothing happens

5. Electron hits atom: can there be leftover KE after excitation? Why?
there is leftover KE from electron excitation as electron KE is a continuous variable
