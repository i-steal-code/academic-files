1. Two parallel wires carry current in the same direction. Attract or repel? Why (one sentence)?
attract. by right hand grip rule, magnetic fields are going in same rotational direction for both wires, so at point of intersection of magnetic fields, the poles are unlike and hence attract.

2. F = Bqv sinθ. For what θ is F = 0? For what θ is F max?
since sin(theta) is a coefficient of Bqv, F = 0 when sin(theta) = 0 when theta = 0, and F max when sin(theta) = 1 when theta = pi/2. This is a mathematical representation of vector resolution of the vector in the direction perpendicular to the field in the direction dictated by FLHR.

3. Write the RC discharge law for I(t). What is τ?
tau is RC constant for capacitor discharge. discharge follows I = I_0 e^-tau/t

4. State Kirchhoff junction rule and loop rule in one line each.
current at junctions = 0, current in loop calculated using resistance within loop and p.d of circuit

6. At potentiometer balance, what two quantities are equal? Why does the galvanometer read zero?
p.d. across R1 = emf of test circuit. 0 current flowing through because there is 0 p.d. across the test circuit when the p.d. across R1 is equal and opposite to the emf of the test circuit. 

7. Write three expressions for energy stored in a capacitor.
energy in capacitor U = 1/2 * QV^2 and other forms derived by substitution with C = QV

8. Ohmic vs non-ohmic: how do you read R from an I-V graph in each case?
ohmic resistor are resistors with constant resistance while non-ohmic may have variable resistance under changing current. ohmic resistors are linear and straightforward, so just get a single point on the graph/gradient of a graph to work out everything. for non-homic resistors, you will have to determine R using V = IR at every juncture since V is no longer linear on the I-V graph. 

9. Velocity selector: E perp B. Condition for undeflected path? Expression for v.
B-field provides magnetic force required to neutralise E-field force (FLHR + B field force formula). when velocity (current) of a charge is not enough, it does not have enough magnetic force to withstand the deflection of the E-field and is not selected by the velocity selector. fully undeflected by the velocity selector when magnetic force = electric force of E-field. Bqv = q (-dv/dr) and then you do some math to get the answer

10. Sliding bar reaches steady speed on rails in B. What two forces balance?
gravitational force (sloped component) and magnetic force (sloped component opposite to gravitational force). N1L, net force = 0 for constant velocity.
