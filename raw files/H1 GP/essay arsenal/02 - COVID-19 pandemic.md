# Case 2 — COVID-19 (all-sector stress test)

**Engine:** Not “a health event that also affected society.” A simultaneous test of science, the state, the economy, the household, and the truth.

**Use for:** almost any title — but only if you name **which system cracked**. “COVID changed everything” scores nothing.

**In 2026:** do not retell 2020. Use the **mechanism that is still true**, and land on a hangover (learning loss, inflation, remote work, polarisation).

**Memorise:** 9 facts, 1 mechanism, **3 pairs**. Forks are lookup.

---

## A. Plot

Early 2020: novel coronavirus, pandemic. Governments chose among bad options: close to save hospitals, or stay open and watch them fail. **BioNTech/Pfizer** and **Moderna** mRNA vaccines arrived fast (BioNTech developed the first; Pfizer manufactured and distributed). WHO ended the international emergency in 2023. The social bill did not end.

COVID made hidden dependencies visible: just-in-time chains with no slack; underpaid care work; schools as childcare; migrant labour in dorms; experts who had never had to be trusted at this scale.

**Mechanism:** A fast airborne shock forces trade-offs in public. Pre-existing cracks (class, age, housing, digital access, trust) decide who pays. Science can make a vaccine faster than society can agree what the vaccine *means*.

---

## B. Nine facts

1. Pandemic 2020; WHO emergency ended 2023. No death total needed.
2. Core trade-off: lives/hospitals vs livelihoods/liberty.
3. mRNA (BioNTech/Pfizer and Moderna): science can sprint; public trust may not.
4. Supply chains snapped (PPE, chips, shipping). Just-in-time failed; inflation followed.
5. School closures → learning loss, mental health, childcare crisis.
6. Inequality: professionals Zoomed; essential workers (and SG migrant workers) took infection.
7. Singapore: Circuit Breaker (Apr 2020); TraceTogether/SafeEntry; Jobs Support Scheme; later “living with COVID.”
8. Singapore’s tell: dormitory outbreak — public health as class geography.
9. After: emergency powers, mandate wars, conspiracy, expert distrust — runway into the polarised 2020s.

---

## C. Three pairs (memorise these)

| Pair | EG1 | EG2 | Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class geography | SG migrant-worker dorms | Laptop professionals on Zoom | Same pathogen, different housing/shifts decide who dies — **so long as** cheap labour is stored in density |
| Science vs legitimacy | mRNA vaccines at historic speed | Vaccine refusal in highly schooled publics | Tools sprint; trust does not — **when** messaging projects certainty it does not have |
| Hangover | Stimulus + bottlenecks → inflation | Mandate culture wars → tribal politics | Emergency trains distrust and kitchen-table rage **after** the virus recedes |

Do not retell 2020. Land on the hangover or the published crack.

## D. Forks (one beat each)

| Topic | Land here |
|---|---|
| Health / science | mRNA = cumulative research + wartime funding, not magic. Allocation (who gets the shot) is ethics. Rely on the method; not on every press conference. |
| Government / liberty | States can still command the body. TraceTogether: competence does not cancel the liberty question. China zero-COVID then abrupt 2022 exit: capacity without adaptability is brittle. The hangover (distrust) is part of the cost. |
| Economy | Laptops vs lungs. Stimulus + bottlenecks = the 2022–24 till. Crises accelerate platforms already in position. SG: Jobs Support kept payrolls; dorms showed growth stored in crowding. |
| Education / youth | Home-based learning is a parent-and-broadband exam. School is a social institution; you cannot Zoom a childhood. |
| Elderly / family | Age-stratified death; isolation as policy. Apps as accidental age discrimination. SG: rhetoric of filial piety vs domestic workers as the hidden care system. |
| Media / truth | Infodemic. Two failures: conspiracy, **and** official overconfidence walked back (airborne spread, masks). Institutions must show their working while science is moving. |
| Tech | Pulled forward telemedicine, remote work, e-commerce. Dark twin: screens, monopoly, tracing mission-creep (retention, not whether apps work). |
| Arts | Live performance died first, recovered last. “Non-essential” is a budget label, not a human one. |
| Environment | 2020 emissions dip, then rebound. Lockdown is not climate policy — societies will not accept turning the economy off. Skip wildlife anecdotes. |
| IR / globalisation | Vaccine nationalism; COVAX was the apology. Viruses ignore sovereignty. Friend-shoring after COVID is cousin to later wartime decoupling. SG: the hub froze. |
| Ethics | Liberty vs duty not to infect. Closures protected grandparents and harmed pupils — a real trade-off. COVID mapped whose crowding we had already accepted. |
| Singapore (“your society”) | Circuit Breaker = a state that can shut a city. TraceTogether = a state that can operationalise science. Dorms = the growth model we had decided not to see. Pair the first two with the third or it is PR. |

## E. Join to US 2024

COVID taught voters to treat experts, foreigners, and “for your own good” as a suspect genre. 2024 spent that lesson on an America still funding two wars.

## F. Exam mistakes

- “COVID showed health matters.” Empty.
- Hero-scientist fanfiction that skips distribution, trust, and class.
- Using only Western lockdowns.
- Death-toll tennis.
- Last date 2020. Land on learning loss, inflation, hybrid work, or polarisation.
