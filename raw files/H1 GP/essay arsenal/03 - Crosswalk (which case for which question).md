## Openers (fill the blanks)

1. **Stress test:** “[Event] did not invent [problem]; it removed the luxury of ignoring it.”
2. **Unequal cost:** “The shock was shared; the bill was not. [A] got inconvenience; [B] got catastrophe.”
3. **Trade-off:** “Not villainy: a choice between [good 1] and [good 2], then frozen into a moral identity.”
4. **Architecture:** “[System] was built to maximise [metric]; [metric] is not the public good in the title.”
5. **Hangover:** “The emergency ended. The personality it trained — [distrust / screens / inflation] — did not.”
6. **Small state:** “[Big actor] decided [X]. Singapore did not get a vote. It got the price.”
