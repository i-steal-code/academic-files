# How to use this arsenal

Three heavy cases beat thirty trivia items. Every deployment must obey `05 - Paper 1 house model.md`.

**Per paragraph:** TS (keywords) → mechanism → **two examples** → principle from the pair → qualifier → implication → link.

**Terminal so-what** = Insight → Qualifier → Implication.  
Not a longer prediction chain. See `04`.

---

## Universal lenses (map onto SALT)

These are not topics. They are the *principles* you extract after two examples.

1. Trade-offs, not villains (competing goods).
2. Stress tests reveal cracks; they rarely create them.
3. Costs are unequally distributed (stakeholder split).
4. Trust is the scarce resource.
5. Architecture beats character (design, incentives, institutions).
6. Mobilisation ≠ sustainability (short run vs long run).
7. Incidental goods vs designed outcomes.
8. Small states live inside other people’s decisions (level: national vs global).
9. Limits ≠ exceptions (*how far* questions).
10. No “solutions” — evaluate against a yardstick.

## When to use which case

Timed chooser: `03`. Spine + **one** cousin sentence. Do not tour two cases.

| Flavour | Spine |
|---|---|
| Democracy, polarisation, media, truth, populism, IR, war | US 2024 |
| Science, health, work, education, elderly, inequality, digitalisation | COVID |
| Effectiveness / “enough” | COVID or SG policy — yardstick first |
| Your society | `06` first; global case only as imported weather |
