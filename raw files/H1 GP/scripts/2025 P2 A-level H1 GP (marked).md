## Q1 — “fever pitch” [1]

**Model:** peak / unprecedented intensity of **public interest** in AI.

**Self:** emphasise how sensational AI has become due to higher intellect.

**Why 0/1:** intensity noun is *pitch* (the top of a rise), context is *interest*, not “AI is smart / sensational”.

## Q2 — two negative impacts [2]

**Model (any 2):** jobs obsolete → unemployment; overreliance → loss of creative thinking; systems cleverer than creators → seize control. Cause **and** effect.

**Self:** labour obsolete; danger of higher intellect and choosing to rule.

**Why 2/2:** two of three payloads. Overreliance unused.

## Q3 — language showing disapproval of the tech industry [3]

**Self:** fat cats = sleazy; obsession = greedy profit; bombard = flood users for profit / immoral.

**Model:** fat cats = rich, privileged, self-serving; obsession = excessive, unhealthy commercial excitement; bombards = aggressive, relentless, overwhelm with unnecessary content. All three serve **self-gain**.

**Why 2/3:** circular “sleazy like a fat cat” misses wealth/privilege. Sermon (“immoral”) is extra; the word’s job is *relentless overwhelm*.
