## 1. Ageing and who is responsible (2026 DHS)

**Question.** *How far do you agree that the government bears the greatest responsibility in meeting the needs of an ageing population in your society?*

**What this trains.** A comparative-responsibility title in "your society" form: name who else could bear the need (families, employers, the person who is ageing), then say why the government still does or does not bear the greatest share. Name the group a Singapore policy misses. Do not list schemes as proof that the state has done enough.

**Open.** `09` §L (a crisis of care, not of planning), A15, `09` §F (SIRDEC).

**Keyword to land.** *greatest responsibility* and *your society*.

**Stand (condition, not a degree).**

**Unpack (stakeholders, who else could be responsible, yardstick for "meeting the needs").**

## 3. Global challenges and cooperation (2026 RVHS)

**Question.** *'Global challenges can only be solved through global cooperation.' How far do you agree?*

The rebuttal must show a challenge that was met, or could only be met, by something other than agreement among states.
