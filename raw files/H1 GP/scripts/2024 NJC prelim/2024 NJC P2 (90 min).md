Shopping malls were once the central hub of the community, a all-in-one place for retail, running errands and socialisation. In the modern day, the scenery has shifted drastically, and what stood in the past may have been eroded by time. This is more so applicable to Singapore, where the fast-paced, modern and pragmatic society ruthlessly roots out that which is deemed as inefficient. Therefore, i stand in disagreement at a large extent to the claim that shopping malls are still relevent today, especially so in singapore.

The advent of online retail, commonly known as e-commerce, has eroded the "economical foundation" of shopping malls today. The author in passage 1 claims in line 3 that "amazon gutted brick-and-mortar shops". I agree with the author's claim and believe it to be widely applicable to singapore. Singapore is a ruthlessly pragmatic and fast-paced society that determines value by efficiency, and the addition of e-commerce to the equation has tipped the scales. In less than a decade over the course of the COVID-19 pandemic in singapore where the circuit breaker dismantled brick-and-mortar shops, e-commerce took up its sword and led the charge for revitalisation of the retail sector where people could no longer visit in-person stores. The rise of popular and now extremely large e-commerce giants in singapore such as shoppee, lazada and carousell boast unparalleled volume that trump brick-and-mortar stores, even after the circuit breaker ended. The sheer efficiency of e-commerce platforms allow one to perform a purchase in less time, with often cheaper prices, anytime, anywhere that has an internet connection. This unbeatable convenience leaves physical stores, especially whole groups of stores in shopping malls, with significantly less business as the pragmatic nature of singaporean society results in many opting to purchase online instead. The past where commerce could only be performed in-person bolsters the model of the shopping mall, where one could purchase all their required goods in one location. But in the present, where all this can be done from the comfort of your bed at home, does anyone even want to travel to the shopping mall for retail anymore? Therefore, so long as there is an option for retail that is more convenient than shopping malls, shopping malls will no longer stay relevant today - more so in the fast-paced pragmatic society of singapore.

On top of eroding in its status as the retail hearthstone, shopping malls are losing on the front that they serve as communual hubs.
