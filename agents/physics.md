- Plan by one finished outcome per day, not hour counts. The user dropped hour counts on 2026-11-07 as arbitrary, and left the earlier physics bootcamp plan after 2 days to work on their own terms.

No decay; Weightage and Outlook sections dropped at the user's request (diversify mastery, not min-max). Since 2026-11-09 the prediction is a lowball by the user's request (a bad day lands near it; all uncertainty points up). It blends two lowballs, weighted by the marks of evidence behind each. The paper lowball is the worst recent full timed P2 in `PAPERS` (54/75, 2025 RI; NYJC, ACJC and RVHS "56+" taken as 56). The chapter lowball is Σ weight × chapter score, where a tested chapter scores `taper.lo / total` (low end of its first attempt, all marks) and an untested one the band floor (4: 85%, 3: 65%, 2: 40%, 1: 10%). The lucky-day ceiling is Σ weight × the content-only band top (100/84/64/39%). Add every new full timed P2 (Wed, Thu) to `PAPERS`. The 2026 RI prelim P2 (17/75) is excluded as outdated. At 9 Nov the lowball is 69.7% (about 52/75) and the ceiling 88.3%. After each taper entry, set the chapter's `taper` field including `lo` (plus `of`, the marks attempted, when only part of a chapter was done, as for Ch 16 capacitance; the untried marks keep the band floor), then update `LEAKS` and `UPDATED` in the page's script and republish the same file path.
- Exam-first priority (user, 2026-11-10): practice sets expose leaks, but how often the exam tests a leak decides its priority. Drill routinely tested calculation and explanation types; don't spend time on niche lecture-note wording. The running log's correction sheet is tiered by the number of 2026 prelim sets (of 6) testing each topic: Tier 1 drill (3+ sets, or habits), Tier 2 reread once (1–2), Tier 3 skim (not seen). Tier new items the same way.
